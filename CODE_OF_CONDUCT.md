# Code of conduct

pier follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep criticism about the code,
not the person. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks aren't tolerated in issues,
pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer,
[@StepanBlaha](https://github.com/StepanBlaha), privately through the email address on their GitHub
profile. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, lock threads or block
people who break these rules.
